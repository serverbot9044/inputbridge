# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want InputBridge to be a welcoming, constructive project for everyone who participates. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to treat one another with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be thoughtful, helpful, and open to feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, never people.
- Respect different experiences, identities, and levels of technical knowledge.
- Report security concerns privately instead of publishing exploit details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and the publication of private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this code.
